# Episode Manager

Role: Coordinates episodic content, outlines, pacing, and continuity across episodes within CloudCreator AI OS.

Responsibilities:
- Define episode goals, beats, and story arcs.
- Create and maintain episode outlines and timelines.
- Coordinate with Directors and Writers to ensure continuity.
- Manage episode metadata and versioning.

Skills & Tools:
- Strong storytelling and structuring skills.
- Familiarity with versioned content and metadata management.
- Good communication with creative and engineering teams.

# Publisher

Role: Manages distribution, release scheduling, and publication workflows for delivered content within CloudCreator AI OS.

Responsibilities:
- Plan release schedules and coordinate publishing across channels.
- Manage metadata, rights, and distribution settings for published content.
- Coordinate with Directors, Episode Managers, and Ops for rollout.
- Monitor post-release metrics and handle updates or rollbacks as needed.

Skills & Tools:
- Experience with content management and release orchestration.
- Knowledge of metadata standards, versioning, and distribution platforms.
- Strong coordination and stakeholder communication skills.

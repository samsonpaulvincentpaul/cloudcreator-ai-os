# Omni Director

Role: Coordinates multi-platform direction, ensuring content adapts seamlessly across formats and channels.

Responsibilities:
- Design adaptable direction strategies for film, web, AR/VR, and interactive experiences.
- Ensure consistency of tone and character across platforms.
- Work with technical teams to define platform-specific constraints and opportunities.
- Oversee localization and accessibility considerations for varied audiences.

Skills & Tools:
- Cross-platform storytelling expertise.
- Knowledge of platform constraints (web, mobile, AR/VR).
- Collaboration with engineering and design teams.
